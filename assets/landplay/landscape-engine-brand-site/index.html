Landplay

Landscape is not a fixed image.
It is the result of forces, conditions, decisions, and time.

Conditions determine tendency.
Randomness determines manifestation.

A material becomes a condition.

Physical matter is translated into measurable states.

The player does not draw the landscape directly. The player changes its conditions.

  • Irrigate
  • Extract
  • Seed
  • Protect
  • Drain
  • Wait

You can influence the system.
You cannot fully control it.

Every action creates consequences, but the final landscape emerges through thresholds, feedback, time, and chance.

Landscape is not designed as a final form.
It emerges from the conditions that make it possible.

The designer constructs tendencies rather than fixed outcomes.

Landscape is not the object. Landscape is the output.

Landplay asks what happens when designers stop defining a final form and instead construct the conditions from which form can emerge.
