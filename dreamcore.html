Dream Core Landscape

First-Semester Project

Project Overview

Dream Core Landscape is a first-semester visual experiment exploring dreamlike environments, artificial nature, and atmospheric world-building. The project includes a short dream story video and an image-to-3D Gaussian Splatting study that transforms generated imagery into immersive spatial scenes.
