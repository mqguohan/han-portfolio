KANANA

Undergraduate Project

Project Overview

KANANA is an undergraduate public welfare brand project about banana biodiversity conservation. Through visual identity, educational storytelling, children's workshops, and a market booth, the project uses the pop-up concept "Banana Tribe" to communicate ecological equality and the message of "All Life Matters."

Market Booth

A pop-up booth presenting the KANANA brand through public interaction, visual materials, educational display, and small participatory activities.

04

Reflection

KANANA connects ecological communication with playful public interaction, using branding as a way to make biodiversity issues more approachable. As an earlier undergraduate project, it reflects my long-term interest in ecology, visual systems, and participatory forms of design.
