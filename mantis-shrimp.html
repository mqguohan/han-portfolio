Mantis Shrimp Umwelt /
Second Nature

Project Overview

My project focuses on the mantis shrimp's unique perception of light and color, reconstructing its multispectral visual experience of the environment. It aims to help humans imagine and approach a completely different world, fostering a deeper curiosity and awareness toward non-human perspectives of nature.

Developed within the context of Second Nature, the project also responds to the course's broader question of how landscapes can be represented when the boundary between the artificial and the natural becomes increasingly ambiguous. Instead of depicting nature as a stable or purely human-centered scene, this project uses AI-based imaging, video, and storytelling to imagine a virtual environment shaped by non-human perception, sensory difference, and ecological complexity.
