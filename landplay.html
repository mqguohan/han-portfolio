Project Overview

LANDPLAY is a playable landscape system where players control environmental conditions and the system responds by generating evolving synthetic landscapes. Instead of giving players total control over a final image, the project lets them influence conditions, tendencies, and system behavior, allowing landscapes to emerge through interaction, randomness, memory, and ecological logic.

Concept

Conditions determine tendency. Randomness determines manifestation.

LANDPLAY is not designed as a tool for producing one controlled final image. It is a game-like system where the player makes decisions, the environment responds, and the final landscape emerges from accumulated conditions.

04

Gameplay Structure

  1. Start
  2. Land Selection
  3. Simulation
  4. Final Reading
  5. Ending World

The player begins by entering the game, selecting a land condition, and interacting with the landscape through different actions. Each action changes local conditions and contributes to global metrics. At the end of the simulation, the system generates a final reading, a world prompt, and an ending landscape.

Reflection

LANDPLAY is not only a visual generator, but a playable landscape research instrument. It explores how landscapes can emerge from conditions, actions, memory, and uncertainty, turning environmental systems into something players can feel, test, and interpret.
